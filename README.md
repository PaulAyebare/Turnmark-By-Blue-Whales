Turnmark by Blue Whales
Turnmark is an offline coaching log for iPhone and iPad. Coaches can plan pool and land workouts, time several swimmers in a heat, and share an individual swimmer’s progress with their family. Turnmark was developed in consultation with Blue Whales Swim Club in Wakiso, Uganda.

Getting started
Turnmark runs on iOS 17 or later and iPadOS 17 or later. You can enter swimmers, run timers, and review results without an account or an internet connection.
1. Open Swimmers → Add swimmer. Enter the swimmer’s name and age, then select the strokes they race. To update a swimmer later, open their profile and tap Edit.
2. Choose Training sessions, Land training, or Swim meets on the home screen. For pool sessions and meets, enter the venue and the pool length. Turnmark uses the pool length to determine where to record splits.
3. After recording results, return to Swimmers and open a name to review that swimmer’s history.
At pool practice
Create a dated session, open a saved workout, or duplicate a session to reuse its sets. Select the swimmers attending and use the lane menu to assign lanes 1–8. The highlighted drill shows the current round and repetition.

Tap Start, then tap each swimmer’s button as they reach the indicated mark. Keep the timing screen open while recording. Turnmark keeps the display awake during active timing. The Recovery tempo clock repeats at 30, 60, 90, or 120 seconds. Use the interval menu to change its length or the restart control to begin a fresh interval.
At a swim meet
Enter the races, heats, strokes, distances, and swimmers. The first swimmer selected establishes the age category for that race. A swimmer’s meet specializations determine which strokes they can enter. You can add an eligible swimmer you missed when you open the race before timing.

Open the meet to see its Race programme. Touch and hold a race card to move it up or down, or tap whichever heat is called. Confirm the swimmers and lanes, tap Start, and record each swimmer’s turns and finish. For a 100 m race in a 25 m pool, the marks are 25, 50, 75, and 100 m. In a 50 m pool, they are 50 and 100 m.

After the final touch, tap Back to races. A completed card stays in the programme and opens its results. All meet results and exports opens the results for the whole meet.
On land
Open Land training to build a workout, add exercises, and set the planned work. During the session, select a swimmer’s set to log what they completed. Tap a logged set to correct it.
Progress reports and exports
Open Swimmers → swimmer’s name and select Training or Meets. Compare finishes for the same event and pool length. For example, look at a swimmer’s 100 m freestyle history in a 25 m pool to see how their times have changed across comparable swims.
What you need	Where to find it
A report for one family	Open the swimmer’s profile → Share progress · family or coach → Family. Choose the period, add an optional coach’s note, then Preview and share PDF.
A coaching report	Follow the same steps and choose Coach. Select all history, the last 30 days, or the last 90 days.
Timing data for analysis	Open session or meet results and export the CSV for a spreadsheet or the TXT for a readable timing log.
A copy of the entire team for safekeeping	Use Backup and restore on the home screen. The restorable archive is a JSON file.


Parents can read a shared PDF without installing Turnmark. To request a report or correct a swimmer’s information, contact the coach who maintains the team’s records.
Storage, privacy, and moving devices
The team archive is stored on the device where the coach enters the data. Turnmark does not automatically sync a team between an iPhone and iPad. The support inbox cannot look up a swimmer’s records. When you export or share a file, you choose its destination and recipients.

To back up or move a team: Open Backup and restore → Save backup to Files. Save the JSON somewhere you can access independently of the app. On the receiving device, open Backup and restore → Restore from Files, choose the JSON, review the team counts, and confirm.

Restoring replaces the receiving device’s current team archive. Back up that device first if it already has records you want to keep. Keep a separate backup before deleting the app or changing devices. A swimmer’s PDF and the CSV or TXT timing exports cannot restore a team.
Troubleshooting
Why is a swimmer greyed out for a heat? Check the age category established by the first entry and the swimmer’s meet specializations. Update the swimmer’s profile if their age or specializations have changed. Pool training allows swimmers to practise other strokes.

I tapped the wrong swimmer. Can I fix the split? During timing, check the swimmer and mark named on Undo before tapping it. For a saved capture, open the results and select the recorded touch to edit it. Enter cumulative elapsed time from the start, between the surrounding captures.

Why is the progress report empty? Check the swimmer, Training or Meets selection, event, pool length, and date range. Choose All history to include older finishes. A swimmer with entries but no recorded finishes may have no times to compare yet.

What happens when I delete a swimmer or session? Deleting a swimmer also removes their recorded times. Deleting a pool session or meet removes its timing results. Back up first if you may need those records again.
