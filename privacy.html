Privacy policy

Turnmark stores the team’s coaching records locally on the iPhone or iPad where the coach enters them.

Information in the app

A coach can enter swimmer names, ages, optional profile details such as height and weight, stroke specializations, workouts, meet entries, lanes, times, and results. Turnmark uses these records to run sessions, compare performances, and prepare reports. The app works without an account and does not send the roster or results to the developer. It includes no advertising or third-party analytics.

Sharing and backups

The coach chooses when to export a report or save a backup, where to put the file, and whom to share it with. A family PDF covers one swimmer. A team backup can include every swimmer’s records. Files saved to a cloud drive, sent by email, or included in a device backup are handled by the service you choose.

Access, corrections, and deletion

Coaches can edit or delete swimmer profiles in the app. Parents and swimmers should contact their coach about access, corrections, or removal. The developer cannot retrieve a team’s local records. Deleting data in Turnmark does not erase copies previously exported or backed up.

This website and support email

This site sets no tracking cookies and includes no analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process ordinary connection information when it serves a page. If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send so we can respond. Please leave swimmer names and team backups out of support requests.
