Getting started

  1. On an iPhone or iPad running version 17 or later, open Swimmers and add a swimmer.
  2. Choose Training sessions, Land training, or Swim meets from Home. For pool sessions and meets, set the pool length.
  3. After timing, open a swimmer’s profile to review their results or share a progress report.

Common questions

Why is a swimmer greyed out for a heat?

The first swimmer selected establishes the age category. Check the swimmer’s age and meet specializations in their profile.

Can I correct a split?

During the effort, use Undo for the most recent touch. To correct a saved capture, open the results, select the touch, and enter the elapsed time from the start.

How do I move the team to another device?

Open Backup and restore → Save backup to Files. Transfer the JSON file, then choose Restore from Files on the receiving device. Restoring replaces that device’s team records. Save a backup of them first.

How does a parent get a report?

The coach opens the swimmer’s profile, chooses Share progress · family or coach → Family, then previews and shares the PDF. Families can read it without installing Turnmark.
